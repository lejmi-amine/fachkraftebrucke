Privacy Policy

1. Privacy at a Glance

General Notes

The following notes provide an overview of what personal data we collect and how we use it. Personal data is all information that relates to an identified or identifiable natural person.

2. Responsible Party

Responsible for data processing within the meaning of the data protection laws is Fachkräftebrücke GmbH, E-Mail: kooperationen@fachkraftebrucke.de.

3. Collected Data

We only collect personal data if you provide it to us yourself, e.g. in the contact form. This includes name, e-mail address, subject and your message.

4. Purpose of Processing

Data processing takes place to answer your inquiry, to communicate with you and to fulfill your desired service. Your data will not be used for other purposes without your consent.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

On this website we use a cookie banner that stores your consent in LocalStorage. No tracking cookies are set unless you expressly accept them.

6. External Services

For the display of fonts and icons we use external providers such as Google Fonts and Font Awesome. When loading these resources, data is transmitted to the respective providers.

7. Contact Form

If you use the contact form, your entries will be stored for the purpose of contacting you and processed by e-mail. The data will be deleted after completion of processing, unless legal retention periods apply.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to information, correction, deletion, restriction of processing and data portability. In addition, you can object to the processing of your data.

Important: This privacy policy is a sample text and does not replace legal review. Please check your specific data processing and adapt the declaration to your actual services.
