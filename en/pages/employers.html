The shortage of skilled workers is real

Nationwide, more and more nursing staff are needed – not only to replace retiring colleagues, but additionally. According to current projections, a nationwide nursing emergency threatens already in this decade.

Fachkräftebrücke offers you a sustainable solution: We tap into the potential of highly motivated, well-trained healthcare professionals from Tunisia – and relieve you of all administrative hurdles.

Your advantages with Fachkräftebrücke

Pre-qualified candidates

We check professional qualifications, work experience, German language skills and personal suitability – before you even see a CV.

Language preparation in Tunisia

All candidates complete an intensive German course (B1/B2) with medical terminology – even before entry.

Legally secure recognition

We take over the entire recognition process of professional qualifications and take care of visas, work permits and official documents.

Integration support

We support the skilled workers with apartment search, official visits and cultural onboarding – for a successful start in your house.

Legal security

All contracts and processes comply with German labor, residence and social security law. No gray areas.

Relief for your HR department

We coordinate the entire process – you focus on the essentials: your patients and residents.
