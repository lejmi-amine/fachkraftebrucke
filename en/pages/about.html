From Skilled Workers for Skilled Workers

We are like you

Two physiotherapists from Tunisia who dared to jump to Germany – and now help others to successfully take the same path.

Our Story

In 2021, we, Marouene and Amine, were exactly where you might be today: In Tunisia, with a completed physiotherapy degree, good career prospects – but the desire to achieve more, to develop further and become part of the German healthcare system.

We set out on the journey. Learned German, had our diplomas recognized, filled out visa applications, found our way in a new culture. It wasn't always easy – but it was the best decision of our lives.

Today, five years later, we both work successfully in Germany, have continued our education, made friends, found a new home. And time and again we received inquiries from colleagues from Tunisia: "How did you do it? Can you help me?"

Going the way together

"We went through the process ourselves – with all the highs and lows. That's why we know exactly what matters."

The bridge we would have wished for back then

Why Fachkräftebrücke?

Back then, we would have wished for someone who not only explains the process to us, but really shows it – from their own experience. Not an agency that just mediates, but people who know how it feels to sit in a German authority for the first time or to take the specialist language exam.

That's why we founded Fachkräftebrücke: We want to close the gap between theoretical mediation and real, practical support. We are not just mediators – we are your colleagues who are already where you want to be.

Our Promise: We treat every applicant the way we would have wanted to be treated ourselves. With respect, transparency and first-hand knowledge.

What makes us

Empathy

We know the fears and questions you have – because we had them ourselves. No anonymous hotline, but real conversations on an equal footing.

Experience

Seven years in Germany, two successful careers, countless visits to authorities – we have not only studied the path theoretically, but lived it practically.

Honesty

We only promise what we can keep. We tell you when something becomes difficult – but also how you can succeed.

Community

Whoever comes to us becomes part of a network. We stay in contact, even after placement – as colleagues, not just as an agency.
